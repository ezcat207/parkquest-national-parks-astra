Photo Credits & Privacy

Real places. Real credit.

A little transparency about the photos and your personal collection.

Your data is yours.

Visits, dates, notes and saved parks stay in localStorage in this browser. We have no account database, advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Hosting providers process ordinary request logs. Exporting creates a file on your device; importing merges a selected backup into this browser. No cloud sync is provided.
